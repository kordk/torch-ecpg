# Contributing to torch-ecpg

Thank you for your interest in `torch-ecpg`! We appreciate the community's support and interest in utilizing this tool for bioinformatics and multi-omics integration.

## Project Context and Bandwidth

`torch-ecpg` is developed and maintained by a small research laboratory. Our primary mandate is conducting scientific research, which means our team operates with limited bandwidth for managing open-source community contributions. 

Currently, our core team is heavily focused on the development of the upcoming 2.0 release, prioritizing the integration of explainable AI features and improved, GPU-accelerated computational backends. 

Because of these constraints, **we are generally unable to review, accept, or maintain large, unsolicited feature contributions.**

## How to Contribute

To ensure that your time is not wasted and to help us manage our workload, please follow these guidelines:

### 1. The "Issue-First" Policy
**Do not open a Pull Request without discussing it first.** If you have an idea for a feature, enhancement, or a significant architectural change, please open an Issue to discuss it with our team. Unsolicited pull requests introducing new features, dependencies, or sweeping changes will likely be closed without review to keep our development roadmap focused.

### 2. Bug Reports
If you discover a bug, please open an Issue. Because this tool is meant for a variety of computing environments, please include the following in your bug report:
* A clear description of the bug and the expected behavior.
* Steps to reproduce the issue.
* Your operating system and Python version.
* Your hardware specifications (specifically your GPU model, CUDA version, and driver details, if applicable).
* Any relevant error logs or tracebacks.

### 3. Submitting Pull Requests
We welcome small, highly targeted Pull Requests, such as:
* Typo corrections in documentation.
* Minor bug fixes (preferably with an associated Issue).
* Small utility updates that do not alter the core architecture.

**If your PR meets these criteria:**
1. Fork the repository and create your branch from `main`.
2. Ensure your code aligns with the existing style and architecture.
3. Open a Pull Request with a clear title and a concise description of the problem it solves. 
4. Link the PR to the relevant Issue (e.g., "Fixes #12").

## Code of Conduct
Please remember that this tool is maintained by a small group of researchers. We expect all interactions in Issues and Pull Requests to remain professional, patient, and respectful of our team's time and scientific objectives.

Thank you for understanding our workflow and for your interest in `torch-ecpg`!
